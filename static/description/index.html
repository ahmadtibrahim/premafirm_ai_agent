Core Features

🤖 AI Sales Assistant (CRM)

AI Chat Box on every CRM lead. Reads full email thread + company notes + contact notes before responding. Draft emails, summarize accounts, suggest next actions.

📧 Smart Email Composer

One-click to push AI draft into Odoo email composer. Auto-fills subject, To field, and threads correctly to CRM lead chatter. No auto-send — Ahmad reviews everything.

📋 Two-Level Note Logging

Company notes (freight needs, lanes, rates, escalation history) vs Contact notes (personal preferences, communication style). Preserved even if contacts change jobs.

🔁 Follow-up Workflow

Daily cron detects no-reply leads. After 3 business days: value-add follow-up draft. After 5 more: urgency follow-up. Cold for 60+ days: reactivation draft. All posted as notes for review.

🏆 Won/Lost Debrief + Checklist

AI-generated debrief posted to company notes on Won or Lost. Won triggers carrier onboarding checklist automatically (carrier packet, insurance, portal, first load, rate con).

📊 Account Summary Generator

One-click AI summary on any company contact: Status, Primary Contact, Lane Interests, Last Activity, Next Action, Risk Flags, Opportunity Score (1–10).

💵 Trip Cost Estimator + Dispatch

Full cost calculation: fuel, driver, maintenance, insurance, weight surcharge. Routes via MapBox. Checks truck availability before dispatch.

🧾 AI Invoice Generator

Analyzes invoice attachments (PDF/image). Extracts BOL, PO, Packing Slip references. Builds service description with route and date. Selects correct service product.

📄 AI Bill Auto-Fill

Processes scanned vendor bills from Documents folder. Extracts vendor, amount, line items, tax, date. Creates draft vendor bill. Learns from every confirmed bill.

🚛 Geotab ELD Integration

Syncs vehicles, telematics (odometer, fuel %, DEF %, GPS), driver profiles, duty status logs, and fuel efficiency. 5-source fuel calculation algorithm.

⛽ IFTA Fuel Log

Auto-created from fuel vendor bills. Detects jurisdiction from postal code, province name, or OCR. Groups by quarter and jurisdiction for IFTA reporting.

📦 POD Generation

Generates PDF Proof of Delivery with compliance info, vehicle details, load specs, stop addresses, and signature fields.

🧠 ML Knowledge Base

Every human decision teaches the AI. Approved drafts (weight 2.0), edited drafts (1.5), rejections with feedback (0.5). Vendor profiles (3.0). Gets smarter over time.

📬 Bulk Email Batching

Send outreach emails to selected leads with configurable delay between each. Templates, scheduling, queue tracking with sent/failed/bounced status per recipient.

🗺️ Google/OSM Address Autocomplete

Address fields on contacts, vehicles, and CRM leads auto-complete via Google Places or Nominatim (free fallback). Fills street, city, province, postal, country automatically.
